## General concept

Event-Carried State Transfer reduces temporal coupling between services by distributing, through events, the subset of state consumers actually need to operate locally. <Cite ids={["bellemare-event-driven-microservices"]} />

Instead of calling the owning service on every read, the consumer maintains a local representation that is updated asynchronously, accepting eventual consistency in exchange for autonomy. <Cite ids={["aws-eventbridge-bus","kleppmann-ddia"]} />

## Quick checklist

### Strong fit

<Checklist items={[
  "Consumers read the same remote data frequently.",
  "A partial local copy solves most use cases.",
  "The team accepts eventual consistency and replay."
]} />

### Warning signs

<Checklist tone="warning" items={[
  "Events carry the producer's whole model without curation.",
  "Local replica ownership is unclear.",
  "Consumers still call the producer for almost everything."
]} />

<Callout title={"Necessary balance"} tone="warning">
  <p>The goal is not to broadcast the whole database through events, but to publish only the state that consciously reduces synchronous dependency. <Cite ids={["bellemare-event-driven-microservices","kleppmann-ddia"]} /></p>
</Callout>

## Common mistakes

- Sending massive payloads with no ownership or evolution plan.
- Failing to version state-carrying events.
- Expecting instant consistency between local replicas.
