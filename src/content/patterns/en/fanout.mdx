## General concept

Fanout becomes useful when one domain event suddenly has several interested parties and each new integration starts introducing accidental dependency back into the producer. <Cite ids={["hohpe-eip"]} />

With a topic or event bus in place, the producer publishes once and lets the messaging layer handle subscription, delivery, and retries for each consumer. <Cite ids={["aws-sns-a2a","aws-sns-fanout-lambda"]} />

## Quick checklist

### Strong fit

<Checklist items={[
  "More than one consumer cares about the same domain fact.",
  "Downstream processing can be asynchronous.",
  "The team wants extensibility without modifying the producer."

### Warning signs

<Checklist tone="warning" items={[

  "Consumers have no idempotency strategy.",

## Common mistakes

- Using fanout when the business flow really requires a sequential process.
- Publishing oversized payloads and turning the bus into a mirror of the producer database.
- Skipping DLQs and replay paths for critical subscribers.
