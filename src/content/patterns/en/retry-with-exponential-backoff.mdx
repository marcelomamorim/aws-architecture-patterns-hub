## General concept

Retry with Exponential Backoff starts from the observation that some failures are temporary, but retrying again immediately and in lockstep usually makes things worse. <Cite ids={["aws-builders-timeouts-retries"]} />

By increasing the delay between attempts and limiting total retries, the client reduces pressure on the dependency while still capturing legitimate quick recoveries. <Cite ids={["aws-pg-retry-backoff","aws-stepfunctions-error-handling"]} />

## Quick checklist

### Strong fit

<Checklist items={[
  "The operation fails in a transient and predictable way.",
  "There is idempotency or another safe repeat mechanism.",
  "Retry policy is centralized and observable."
]} />

### Warning signs

<Checklist tone="warning" items={[
  "Retries are configured independently at several layers of the stack.",
  "The system retries permanent business errors.",
  "There is no jitter and no maximum attempt limit."
]} />

<Callout title={"Golden rule"} tone="warning">
  <p>Retry is not a universal cure: it only works when combined with error classification, idempotency, and a clearly bounded patience budget. <Cite ids={["aws-builders-timeouts-retries","aws-pg-retry-backoff"]} /></p>
</Callout>

## Common mistakes

- Retrying without jitter and causing a thundering herd.
- Retrying non-idempotent operations.
- Using very high maxAttempts with no latency budget.
